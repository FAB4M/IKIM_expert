# Restaurants in Grand World Phú Quốc and the Vinpearl / VinWonders / Vinpearl Safari complex (Bãi Dài, Gành Dầu, northern Phú Quốc)

Research date: 6 Nov 2026. Method note for the report writer: direct page fetches of tripadvisor.com and sunset-town.com were blocked by this session's network policy, so all numbers below come from search-engine result pages and their snippets/summaries of the cited URLs, not from reading the pages directly. Ratings on TripAdvisor differ between regional mirrors (.com, .co.uk, .com.au, .in, .sg) and snapshot dates; where they disagree, both figures are given. Treat every number as "approximately as of the snapshot", and re-check before publishing.

Location codes used inside Grand World (e.g. TH1-09, SA-06-01, DTH02-16, DD1-67) are the complex's own shop-unit codes. Zones mentioned: Shanghai Town (SA-), Mallorca Town (DTH-), Indochine Town (K-Street, MD-/OR-), the Venice canal (TH1-), clock tower / glass bridge area (DD1-).

## Q1: Which restaurants exist in Grand World Phú Quốc (name, cuisine, price, location, status)?

### Takeaway
Grand World is a 24/7 complex with dozens of outlets: canal-side sit-down restaurants (seafood, Chinese, Korean, Indian, Italian/Western) that carry a tourist premium, a cheaper night market and street stalls (30,000–150,000 VND per dish), a Korean "K-Street" in Indochine Town, and a few chains (Lotteria, Bún quậy Kiến Xây's north-island branch). TripAdvisor counts about 81 restaurants within 5 km of Grand World. The places that keep coming up are Mai Hương (seafood), Phú Quý (Chinese/seafood), Crazy Crab (seafood), Maharaja Lounge (vegetarian Indian), Bún quậy Kiến Xây, JMT Korea, Jack's Pizzeria and the night market.

- Canal-side sit-down places serve Vietnamese, Japanese, Korean and European food "in a setting that adds a premium to everything on the menu", with prices well above comparable places in Dương Đông or along Long Beach; street-food stalls run 30,000–80,000 VND per dish; most sit-down restaurants take cards, night-market stalls usually want cash — [Local Vietnam: Grand World Phu Quoc](https://localvietnam.com/phu-quoc/grand-world-phu-quoc/)

**Night market (Chợ đêm Grand World)**
- Open 16:00–02:00 daily, free entry; dishes about 30,000–150,000 VND (Vinpearl figure); another source estimates snacks/drinks 50,000–200,000 VND per item and 200,000–600,000 VND per person for a group meal — [Vinpearl Resort Vietnam: Chợ đêm Grand World](https://vinpearlresortvietnam.com/cho-dem-grand-world-phu-quoc/); [travel.com.vn: Chợ đêm Phú Quốc 2026](https://travel.com.vn/tin-tuc-du-lich/cho-dem-phu-quoc-v18313.aspx)

- Signature items: sea urchin (nhum), còi biên mai (pen-shell muscle), mực nháy/mực chớp (squid), Hàm Ninh crab (ghẹ Hàm Ninh), grilled scallops, battered fried shrimp, squid steamed with fish sauce — [Mytour: Những địa điểm ẩm thực tại Grand World](https://mytour.vn/vi/blog/bai-viet/nhung-dia-diem-am-thuc-tai-grand-world-phu-quoc.html); [Riverside Mini Hotel: Ăn gì ở Grand World](https://riversideminihotel.com/an-gi-o-grand-world/)

### Inferences
- Grand World food falls into three price tiers: (1) night-market and street stalls at about 20,000–150,000 VND per item; (2) mid-range chains and Vietnamese/Korean outlets (Bún quậy Kiến Xây at 50,000–80,000, K-Street, Lotteria); (3) canal-side seafood and "fine dining" restaurants with tourist pricing, where seafood is sold by the kilo.
- Most named outlets were confirmed active in 2025–2026 sources (Vinpearl guide about 5 months old, Trip.com 2026 pages, TripAdvisor reviews into 2026). Places that appear only in older Vietnamese listicles (Sông Hằng, Hải sản Hàm Ninh, B&B Seafood, Bach, Topaz Ruby, Nautilus, Phở Lý Quốc Sư, Đại Quang, Bếp Nhà) could not be confirmed as still open.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm 2026 operating status for Sông Hằng, Hải sản Hàm Ninh, B&B Seafood, Bach Restaurant, Topaz Ruby, Nautilus, Phoenix 84, Đại Quang, Phở Lý Quốc Sư, Bếp Nhà, Cơm Niêu Việt, Bò Tơ Tây Ninh 68 or Pizza Villa. Several of the Vietnamese listicles are 1–3+ years old.
- A TripAdvisor listing note says Mai Hương "has a different name now"; the new name was not found.
- Exact unit codes and hours are missing for most outlets other than the ones in Vinpearl's own guide.
- Phu Zim's cuisine, and Phuquoc123's, were not stated in the retrieved snippets.
