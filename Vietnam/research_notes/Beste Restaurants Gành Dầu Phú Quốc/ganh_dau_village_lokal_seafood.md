## Which restaurants exist here? (inventory: name, location, cuisine, price, hours, status)

### Takeaway
Vietnamese roundups name about 10–12 independent eateries again and again. They cluster on the ĐT45 coast road through Ấp Gành Dầu (Tổ 1–8) and Ấp Chuồng Vích near the Gành Dầu port. The "big three" local seafood names are **Biên Hải Quán, Nhà hàng biển Phúc Ngân and Hoàng Dự Seafood**. Next come **Gió Biển / Sunset Story** (resort-style, group-oriented), **Hải Đăng** (hilltop view), **Gành Dầu Cảng** (port-front), plus cheap spots (**Quán Quen, Cơm gà Hải Huệ, Ốc Bối Bối, Cây Dừa**) and a few cafés and beach bars (**40six Beach Haus, Chill Bistro, Sunset Story café**). Almost all listing data (hours, prices, phones) is 1–5 years old and conflicts between sources. Status in 2026 is unverified for most of them.

### Inferences
- The core village inventory is quite stable across 2019–2025 blogs. Biên Hải Quán, Phúc Ngân, Hoàng Dự, Gió Biển and Hải Đăng appear in almost every Vietnamese roundup.
- Newer additions (2023–2025 TripAdvisor or Facebook IDs) are oriented to Korean or international guests: Maison Gio (TA ID d3399xxxx = listed about 2025), Gành Dầu Cảng (Facebook page ID 6155… = created about 2024) and 40six Beach Haus.
- The Biên Hải Quán phone "0169 984 9214" was a Viettel 11-digit number. Under the 2018 renumbering 0169 became 039, giving **039 984 9214**. This conversion is unverified.
- Gió Biển and Sunset Story are evidently one business with two brands. Wanderlog's "Permanently Closed" flag may mean the old Google listing was merged or renamed rather than the venue closing. Needs on-site or phone confirmation.

- No reliable Gành Dầu-specific bún kèn, bún quậy or bánh canh stall was found. A blog naming "Bún kèn Út Lượm" did not confirm it is in Gành Dầu, and it is more likely in Dương Đông.

## Ratings per platform (Google Maps, TripAdvisor, Foody, Wanderlog, Mindtrip, Trip.com, etc.)

### Takeaway
Village restaurants have **few English-platform reviews**. Biên Hải Quán is the only one with a substantial TripAdvisor count (46). It scores consistently in the **high 3s** (3.6–3.9) across TripAdvisor, Google (per blogs), Trip.com and Wanderlog. Phúc Ngân and Hoàng Dự score around **4.0–4.3** on Google-derived Mindtrip data with 100–280 reviews. Café and bar spots (40six, Gió Biển–Sunset Story) score around 4.4–4.7. TripAdvisor's "Ganh Dau" category (78–87 restaurants) is dominated by VinWonders, Wyndham and Grand World venues, so village places rank only mid-table.

## What do reviews say? (signature dishes, praise, complaints, sunset, value)

### Takeaway
Praise centres on **fresh live seafood picked from tanks** and cooked to order (steamed, grilled or hotpot), **sea and sunset views** and lower prices than Dương Đông. The signature dishes are **nhum nướng mỡ hành** (grilled sea urchin with scallion oil), **ghẹ hấp** (steamed blue crab), **gỏi cá trích** (herring salad), **mực nướng/hấp** (squid) and **lẩu** (hotpot). Recurring complaints are about **Biên Hải Quán**: rude or slow service (tour groups get priority), hygiene (floor litter, dirty toilets), "imported, not local" seafood, and menu or weighed prices not matching. There is also one **food-poisoning report (Cát Biển 2)** and one **lobster price dispute (Vua Hải Sản)**. The universal advice is to **confirm the per-kg price and watch the weighing before cooking**.

## Pick-your-own seafood, floating rafts (bè) and fishing-village eateries

### Takeaway
In Gành Dầu itself, the "pick from the tank, pay by weight" model is standard: Biên Hải Quán, Phúc Ngân, Gió Biển and Gành Dầu Cảng all display live seafood in tanks or pens. Phúc Ngân is the closest to an over-water or raft experience, with fish pens beneath it. **True floating raft restaurants (nhà bè) are in Rạch Vẹm fishing village**, a short drive east, which is a "nearby excursion" rather than Gành Dầu proper. Some guides call it about 15 km away.

### Cited Findings
- Biên Hải Quán: live seafood displayed in tanks and cooked to order — [rootytrip.com](https://rootytrip.com/en/top-9-famous-eateries-and-restaurants-in-ganh-dau/); diners choose their seafood and preparation (steam, fry, hotpot) — [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g12666019-d13138040-Reviews-Bien_Hai_Quan-Ganh_Dau_Phu_Quoc_Island_Kien_Giang_Province.html)
- Phúc Ngân: built over or next to the sea, with small fish pens (lồng nuôi) below — [mindtrip.ai](https://mindtrip.ai/restaurant/ganh-dau-kien-giang/nha-hang-bien-phuc-ngan-hai-san-tuoi-song-ngon-gia-re-gan/re-VFXwI6o2); [buiphuquoc.com](https://buiphuquoc.com/top-cac-quan-an-ngon-view-bien-dep-o-ganh-dau-phu-quoc/) (snippets)

### Inferences
- For a "pick-your-own on the water" experience within walking or short-scooter distance of Chuồng Vích, Phúc Ngân (over the water with pens) and Biên Hải Quán (on the sand, with tanks) are the realistic options. For proper floating rafts, Rạch Vẹm is the destination, and it doubles as the starfish-beach excursion.

### Gaps
- Current (2025–2026) operating status, prices and ratings of the Rạch Vẹm rafts were not researched in depth (out of core scope).
- The distance from Gành Dầu village to Rạch Vẹm is unverified (one source says ~15 km; road distance via ĐT45 and the north coast road may differ).
