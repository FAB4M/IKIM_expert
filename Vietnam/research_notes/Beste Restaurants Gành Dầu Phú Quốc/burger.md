## 1. Which restaurants in the north (Gành Dầu, Bãi Dài, Grand World, VinWonders, Cửa Cạn, Ông Lang) serve burgers, and how good are they?

### Takeaway
There is **no dedicated, well-reviewed burger joint in the Gành Dầu / Bãi Dài / Grand World area**. Burgers up north come from (a) resort and beach-club kitchens (SOL by Meliá's beach shack, The Haven Beach Club, the Sheraton's restaurants) and (b) fast-food chains inside Grand World and VinWonders (Lotteria, Burger King). Evidence on burger quality in the north is thin: single reviews, not patterns. The nearest "proper" burger destinations are on Trần Hưng Đạo in Dương Đông (Winston's, Khói Burger), about 20–30 km / 30–50 min south.

### Inferences
- **Suggested ranking "in the area" (Gành Dầu / Bãi Dài / Grand World), by likely burger quality (evidence is thin throughout):**
  1. **SOL by Meliá beach shack, Wagyu burger** (Bãi Dài): the only northern burger with an explicit "exceptional" review. Single data point.
  2. **The Haven Beach Club** (Bãi Dài, ~2.2 km from Grand World): burgers on the menu, beach-club setting, open to 23:00. No burger-specific reviews found.
  3. **Sheraton Phu Quoc Long Beach (Wok & Curry / Daily Social)** (Bãi Dài): the highest-rated restaurants in Gành Dầu on TA (5.0/69 and ~4.9/~150). Burger evidence is one pork-burger mention at Wok & Curry. Worth asking whether a burger is available à la carte.
  4. **Lotteria Grand World** (Shanghai Town): a dependable but basic Korean-Vietnamese fast-food burger, no park ticket needed, open late.
  5. **Burger King / Lotteria inside VinWonders**: only useful if already inside the park (ticket required). The one park burger review (160,000 VND, "very poor") is a warning sign.
- **On the way north (Ông Lang, roughly halfway to Dương Đông):** Mango Bay (pricey, mixed reviews; sliders liked) and Bông Burger & Drink (promising but only 2 reviews). Happy Bites is closed. Rory's location needs verification.
- A traveller who wants a *really good* burger should plan the trip to Dương Đông (Winston's), see §2. The northern options are convenience picks.
- The Sheraton is called "Long Beach" but sits on Bãi Dài in Gành Dầu commune (TA files it under Gành Dầu), not on Bãi Trường/Long Beach near Dương Đông. Don't confuse the two.

## 2. Best-rated burger places on Phú Quốc overall, distance from Gành Dầu / Grand World, and delivery

### Takeaway
**Winston's Burgers & Beer** (121 Trần Hưng Đạo, Dương Đông) is the island-wide consensus pick: TA 4.5 from ~1,950 reviews, Google ~4.4, Australian-beef patties on homemade buns, burgers about 90,000–370,000 VND. From Grand World it is roughly **20–25 km / 30–50 min by car or Grab**. Premium wagyu burgers exist at the southern resorts (Pullman's Mad Cow 590,000 VND; Premier Residences' Blue Burger 390,000 VND), but those sit 40–55+ km from Gành Dầu. Food-delivery coverage of the Grand World/Gành Dầu area is **not confirmed**.

### Inferences
- **Suggested ranking "worth the drive" (island-wide):**
  1. **Winston's Burgers & Beer** (Dương Đông): best-documented burger on the island (TA 4.5/~1,950; Google ~4.4/~1,382). About 21–25 km / 35–50 min from Grand World, since Trần Hưng Đạo runs south from central Dương Đông; this is an estimate from the Grand World↔Dương Đông figures above.
  2. **Khói Burger** (185 Trần Hưng Đạo, near Winston's): craft-burger option, but very few reviews. Verify it's still open.
  3. **Mad Cow, Pullman** (Long Beach, south): premium wagyu at 590,000 VND. Probably ~35–40 km from Grand World (estimate).
  4. **Pineapple Eatery & Bar / Buddy / Sailing Club** (Dương Đông–Long Beach): good fallback burgers, not specialists.
  5. **Blue Burger at Premier Residences / The Deck** (Bãi Khem, far south): premium, but ~50–55+ km and 70–90 min from Gành Dầu (estimate). Only worth it combined with a south-island day (Sunset Town / Hòn Thơm cable car).
- **Ông Lang**, roughly 12–15 km / 20–25 min from Grand World (estimate, not sourced), is the nearest cluster of Western places on the way south.
- Ordering Winston's via GrabFood to Grand World is plausible but unverified. Given ~20–25 km, expect long delivery times, high fees or out-of-range errors. Burgers travel poorly over 40+ minutes anyway.

### Gaps
