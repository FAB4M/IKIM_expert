# Resort and Hotel Restaurants, Bars and Beach Clubs plus Independent International Restaurants, Bãi Dài / Gành Dầu (north-west Phú Quốc)

Research date: 6 November 2026. Method note: in this session, direct page fetches of TripAdvisor, Google Maps, Restaurant Guru, Wanderlog, Booking.com, Agoda, wyndhamhotels.com, ihg.com and radissonhotels.com were all blocked by the network proxy. Every number below therefore comes from search-engine result snippets or summaries of those pages, not from a live page view. TripAdvisor snippets come from different regional mirrors (.com, .co.uk, .com.au, .in, .sg) and different snapshot dates, so review counts often differ by a few percent. Where snapshots conflict, both values are given.

## 1. Which resorts and hotels are on the Bãi Dài – Gành Dầu coast now (incl. rebrandings), and which have restaurants worth visiting?

### Takeaway
The non-Vinpearl international-brand resorts on Bãi Dài / Gành Dầu are now Wyndham Grand Phú Quốc (formerly VinOasis), Sheraton Phú Quốc Long Beach Resort (formerly Vinpearl Resort & Golf Phú Quốc), Crowne Plaza Phú Quốc Starbay and Radisson Blu Resort Phú Quốc. Sheraton has the strongest and best-rated restaurant line-up (Daily Social, Wok & Curry, Il Tramonto). Mövenpick Resort Waverly is not on Bãi Dài: it is at Ông Lang (Cửa Dương), further south. No Hilton exists in the area, a "Phu Quoc Marriott Resort & Spa" is still in the pipeline (2027), and the "Sonata/Best Western" property is Best Western Premier Sonasea, which is in Dương Tơ in the south-west, not Bãi Dài.

### Inferences
- Two of the four "international" Bãi Dài resorts (Wyndham Grand, Sheraton) are former Vinpearl properties, so older reviews (before 2023) under the Vin names describe different operators and menus.
- For an upscale, international or special-occasion dinner away from the Vin cluster, Sheraton (three à-la-carte concepts) and Crowne Plaza (Amber Sands beach club) are the strongest bets. Radisson Blu's offer is thinner (one all-day restaurant plus a lounge and bars).

### Gaps
- No live check was possible of the official dining pages (wyndhamhotels.com, marriott.com, ihg.com, radissonhotels.com were blocked); outlet lists come from search snippets.
- Smaller 3–4-star hotels along Bãi Dài (shophouse hotels, Starbay area) were not inventoried; none surfaced with a notable restaurant.
- Whether the 2027 Marriott/W projects are on Bãi Dài could not be confirmed.

## 2. Restaurant-by-restaurant inventory: cuisine, price level (VND), access for non-guests, signature dishes, sunset/beach-club status

### Takeaway
The best-documented upscale or international outlets are Sheraton's Wok & Curry (Indian/Asian, $$$$), Il Tramonto (Italian, from 12:00) and Daily Social (international market buffet), Crowne Plaza's Amber Sands Beach Club (open-fire beach club, set dinners at 1.3M VND/person) and Wyndham Grand's Atlantis (Italian, wood-oven pizza). Radisson Blu's Avenue/Annex/Azure Bars are serviceable but less distinctive. Sheraton's outlets take public online reservations (SevenRooms). Crowne Plaza markets its beach club to "casual diners", which suggests outside guests are welcome.
