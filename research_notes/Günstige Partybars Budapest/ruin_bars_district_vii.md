# Ruin bars and bar complexes of Budapest District VII (plus adjacent VI / V / IX): fact sheet for a cheap-party group (benchmark: PopoCafe Prague), status as of 7 Nov 2026

## 1. Which venues are open (Nov 2026)? Address, opening hours, closures, relocations, rebrands 2017–2026

### Takeaway
Of the 18 venues on the brief, only about half are still running as bars. **Closed:** Anker't (closed 1 Feb 2020), Mika Tivadar Mulató (closed 2021; the site is being turned into a hotel), Gozsdu Manó Klub (stopped as a club in Aug 2017), Hello Baby (reported permanently closed), Ellátó Kert (reported closed in 2019), and Ellátó at Kazinczy 52/b (farewell party in Nov, most likely 2023). Szóda is probably closed. **Open and relevant for partying:** Instant-Fogas (Akácfa u. 49–51, daily 18:00–06:00; Liebling rooftop bar at the same address), Szimpla Kert (Kazinczy u. 14, to about 04:00), Füge Udvar (Klauzál u. 19–21, daily 16:00–05:00), Doboz (Klauzál u. 10, Wed–Sat 18:00–05/06:00) and, just outside District VII, Morrison's 2 (Szent István krt. 11, District V). **Open but chill (no dancing):** Kőleves Kert, Kisüzem, Csendes Létterem / Csendes Társ (District V), Pótkulcs (District VI), Élesztőház (District IX), Tuk Tuk (a hotel cocktail bar in District VI).

## 2. Current prices (HUF): 0.5 l beer, longdrink, shot, entry/cover, cup deposit, cloakroom (with price status)

### Takeaway
Ruin-bar drinks in District VII cost much more than at PopoCafe. A 0.5 l draught lager is about 1,570–1,760 HUF at Instant-Fogas and 2,200 HUF at Szimpla. Spirits run about 1,880–3,000 HUF per shot or 4 cl, and a simple longdrink at Instant-Fogas is 2,210 HUF on paper (a review reports 2,610–3,120 HUF for a gin and tonic). Many venues add a 300–500 HUF cup deposit. Entry is generally free at Szimpla, Instant-Fogas and Füge. Doboz uses a ticket worth about €5.50–7.50 that includes a drink, and Morrison's 2 is free before 21:00 (except Wed/Thu). The cheapest party prices come from time-limited promotions: Füge's 590 HUF shots before 21:00 and Morrison's 2's 249–299 HUF Monday beers.

## 6. Recurring complaints and pitfalls

### Takeaway
Common complaints by venue:
- **Instant-Fogas:** deposit tokens not handed out, tips added when 0% was chosen, rude staff, physically aggressive bouncers, queues, tourists.
- **Szimpla:** expensive, touristy, queues, dirty toilets, inconsistent staff.
- **Füge Udvar:** a beer jumping from 1,300 to 2,200 HUF at the bar because of the cup deposit, and an overcharge report.
- **Pótkulcs:** a 15% card surcharge (review).
- **Doboz:** a crowded dance floor, an entry fee, and one very high gin and tonic price (7,700 HUF).

Across the district, the cup-deposit system (300–500 HUF, refundable only with cup plus token) and VIP or "mandatory" upsells are the main traps.

### Inferences
- For a budget group, the practical rules are:
  - Keep the cup and token and return both before leaving.
  - Check the bill for added tips or service (Instant-Fogas prices already include 10% service).
  - Carry some cash for deposits and cloakrooms.
  - Expect queues at Instant-Fogas and Szimpla on Fri/Sat after about 22:00–23:00.
