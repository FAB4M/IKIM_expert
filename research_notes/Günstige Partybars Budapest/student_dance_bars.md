# Cheap student party bars and dance bars in Budapest (not the classic big ruin bars), benchmarked against PopoCafe Prague, as of November 2026

Method note (applies to the whole file):
- Research date: 2026-11-07. Direct page fetching was blocked in this session. morrisons2.hu, erasmuslifebudapest.com and xpatloop.com did not resolve, and the proxy refused reddit.com and tripadvisor.com. Every fact below therefore comes from web-search result snippets and summaries of the cited pages, not from reading the full pages. The search budget ran out before Reddit threads or Google Maps mirrors could be checked. Treat prices as "reported by the cited page"; nobody re-checked them on the page itself.
- Price status labels used: **official (source, date)** = venue's own site or price list; **event listing (date)** = organiser listing such as Erasmus Life Budapest (ELB) or ESN; **from review (date)** = a guest review; **third-party guide (date)** = a guide or aggregator; **not found** = no figure located. No prices were estimated or invented.
- Context benchmark: a 0.5 l draught beer in Budapest restaurants and pubs averaged about €2.5, against €2.4 in Prague, according to Numbeo-based 2024 data — [Index 2024-06-20](https://index.hu/gazdasag/2024/06/20/sor-fogyasztas-alkohol-fogyasztas-arak-europai-unio/). One undated tourism page puts domestic 0.5 l beer in downtown bars at roughly 800–900 Ft — [Kingbike](https://kingbike.hu/mennyibe-kerul-egy-sor-budapesten/).

## Q1. Which candidates still exist as of November 2026? Closures, renovations and relocations in 2024–2026

### Takeaway
Most of the named "classic cheap" candidates no longer exist. Vittula closed in May 2025. Gozsdu Manó Klub (2017), Morrison's Opera/Music Pub (March 2018), Corvintető (July 2018), Kuplung, Raqpart and very probably Hello Baby are also gone. Venues confirmed active in 2026 and fitting the profile: Morrison's 2, Stifler Ház, Füge Udvar, Doboz, Instant-Fogas (a big party complex, partly overlapping the "big ruin bar" scope), Ötkert (a club, not cheap) and Retox Bar. Szóda's status is contested. Ráday utca has café-bars and culture bars but no dance bar.

### Inferences
- Of the brief's named list, only Morrison's 2 is both clearly alive in 2026 and squarely "cheap student party bar with dancing". The best alternatives found are Stifler Ház with Füge Udvar next door (free entry), Doboz, and Retox (backpacker scene).
- Zöld Pardon is an open-air summer venue. Even if it operates in 2026, it is almost certainly not open in November. This is an inference from the "weather-dependent" hours and its seasonal history; no 2026 schedule was found.

The viable venues cluster in District VII: Stifler Ház, Füge Udvar, Doboz, Instant-Fogas, Szóda and Lámpás all sit within roughly 500 m of each other. ## Q3. Current prices in HUF (beer, longdrink, shot, entry, cloakroom, deals) with status

### Takeaway
Morrison's 2 is the only venue with documented PopoCafe-level drink prices:
- Monday 21:00–23:00 a pint costs 249–299 Ft.
- Its "regular" beer is reported at about 899 Ft.
- Free entry before 21:00 (except Thursday), or 1,000–1,500 Ft for Erasmus Mondays.
- Unlimited-drink tickets cost 4,000–10,000 Ft depending on arrival time.

Stifler Ház and Füge Udvar have free entry (Füge: 590 Ft shot promo), but their beer prices were not found. Instant-Fogas has free entry but pub-level prices (0.5 l Soproni 1,570 Ft, longdrinks from 2,210 Ft). Ötkert is expensive (4,490–8,000 Ft entry).

## Q5. Party factor: bar vs party bar vs club, dance floors, music, crowd, busy times, door policy

### Inferences
- For a group wanting to start cheap and dance later, a plausible ranking basis is:
  - (a) Morrison's 2 on a Monday (Erasmus night, 249–299 Ft pints, 1,000–1,500 Ft or free entry), or on Fri/Sat with a table booking.
  - (b) District VII crawl: Füge Udvar shot promo before 21:00 → Stifler Ház dance floors and karaoke (free) → Doboz (Wed–Sat, low cover) if they want a more club-like floor.
- Bouncer behaviour at Morrison's 2 is the main reputational risk. The worst reports (pepper spray, assault) come from TripAdvisor reviews of uncertain date; the review ID r850… suggests about 2022 (an inference). Treat this as a recurring theme rather than a confirmed 2026 pattern.
- Cup deposits (Füge, Instant-Fogas) and cloakroom fees (Instant-Fogas 500 Ft; Morrison's 2 "small fee") are recurring hidden costs to flag.
