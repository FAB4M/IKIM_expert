## Q1: Which cheap bars do Hungarian and local sources recommend for students and young people, especially pubs that become dance spots late at night? (Per-venue fact sheets)

### Takeaway
Almost all of the Hungarian "olcsó kocsma" and "egyetemi törzskocsma" lists I could find are old (We Love Budapest 2011–2014, Funzine years old). Several venues they praise for dancing have since closed: Vittula (2025), Auróra, Szóda, and the Ellátó bar on Klauzál tér. The best-supported current candidates for the "cheap + young crowd + spontaneous dancing + no cover" profile are:
- **Füge Udvar** (4 dance floors, free entry, drink promos, mainly Hungarian young crowd)
- **Stifler Ház** (free entry, 4 rooms with different music, 499 HUF happy-hour pints reported in 2026)
- **Gólya** (VIII, cooperative presszó and community house, open until 4:00 on Fri/Sat, active 2026 party calendar)
- **Dzzs Bár** (cheap alternative bar with DJ sets)
- **Morrison's 2** (big student club, free entry before 21:00 and on Tuesdays)

Good options without dancing, or with older information: Pótkulcs, Kakas Presszó, Ördögsarok, Szilvuplé (Latin/salsa dance bar), Ellátó Kert, Krimó.

- In 2020 Erzsébetváros adopted a **midnight closing time** with a strict criteria system. Venues need a special permit to stay open after midnight — [444, 2020-03-04](https://444.hu/2020/03/04/jon-az-ejfeli-zarora-es-a-szigoru-kriteriumrendszer-a-bulinegyedben); [WLB 2020-03-04](https://welovebudapest.com/cikk/2020/03/04/csak-igy-lehetne-nyitva-tartani-ejfel-utan-a-bulinegyedben/)

- Metropol (Sep 2025): residents fear the district is becoming a "playground for pub tourists" (noise, rubbish) — [Metropol 2025-09](https://metropol.hu/aktualis/2025/09/erzsebetvaros-bulinegyed-2)

### Inferences
- Best fit for the PopoCafe profile (cheap drinks, young local crowd, free entry, several dance floors, open until 4–5 am): **Füge Udvar** and **Stifler Ház**. Both sit in District VII but are described by local and student sources as Hungarian-student places rather than tourist magnets. Both list the same 590 HUF/3 cl spirits promo, which may point to a common operator. Shot and longdrink prices still need to be checked on site.
- Best "Geheimtipp" picks, i.e. lesser-known and local:
  - (1) **Gólya** (VIII): cooperative, community-run, alternative crowd, parties until 4 am on weekends. Current drink prices are unknown and some nights are ticketed.
  - (2) **Dzzs Bár**: small, cheap, DJ sets, "more youthful".
  - (3) **Pótkulcs**: hidden-courtyard local kocsma with free programmes. This is a pre-drinking spot, not a dance spot.
  - Szilvuplé is a dance bar but Latin/salsa-oriented, so it is a weaker fit for a pop/party crowd.
- Morrison's 2 is the classic Hungarian student party club. With free entry before 21:00 and on Tuesdays it can work, but it is a bigger club with mixed reviews about drink scams. It fits the "main ranking" better than the "Geheimtipp" slot.
- The classic "kocsma that turns into a dance spot at night" type seems to have shrunk in District VII after the 2020 midnight-closing rules. Several such venues (Vittula, Szóda, possibly Kisüzem's late hours) have closed or been restricted. Late-night dancing now concentrates in licensed bigger venues (Füge, Stifler, Morrison's 2, Instant-Fogas).

## Q2: University club nights open to the public (BME/ELTE/Corvinus, Schönherz, faculty parties)

### Takeaway
University dorm parties such as Schönherz are effectively **not open to outsiders**: a resident has to register you as a guest at the front desk, with photo ID. Public-facing student parties mostly take place in commercial clubs like Morrison's 2, which used to give free entry to students of certain universities. I found no current faculty-party ("kari buli") ticket prices.

## Q3: What Hungarian locals say about the tourist ruin bars (Szimpla, Instant-Fogas) and which alternatives they prefer

### Takeaway
Szimpla is widely called the most touristy ruin bar. It is also clearly the most expensive: **2,200–2,500 HUF per 0.5 l** in 2026, about twice a neighbourhood pub. Instant-Fogas is cheaper (1,570 HUF/0.5 l, free entry), but it is a huge tourist-heavy complex. Alternatives that locals and students name: Füge Udvar, Krimó, Fekete Kutya, Kisüzem, Dzzs, Pótkulcs, Gólya and Dürer Kert.

### Cited Findings
- **Szimpla Kert** prices (*from guide, Hungary Unlocked, updated about Oct 2026*): draft lager **0.3 l 1,900 HUF, 0.5 l 2,200 HUF**; craft 0.5 l 2,500 HUF; non-alcoholic beer 2,000 HUF; cocktails **5,500 HUF**; pálinka 4 cl **3,000 HUF**; single malt 4,000 HUF. Prices can differ by a few hundred HUF between bar counters — [Hungary Unlocked Szimpla guide](https://hungaryunlocked.com/szimpla-kert-budapest-guide/)

## Q4: Local price benchmarks 2025–2026 (korsó beer, fröccs, shot): what counts as cheap, normal and expensive?

In late 2026, a plain 0.5 l draft lager costs about **1,200–1,320 HUF in a neighbourhood pub**, about **1,500–1,600 HUF in big party complexes** (Instant-Fogas), and **2,200–2,500 HUF in Szimpla**. Rooftop bars charge 2,000–3,500 HUF. Retail beer prices rose about 7.6 % year on year (KSH) and about 89–90 % over 5–10 years, and the beer excise tax rose about 10 % from January 2026. Reliable 2026 fröccs and shot benchmarks: **not found**, apart from tourist-venue pálinka (3,000 HUF/4 cl at Szimpla) and old 590 HUF/3 cl promo prices.
