## 1. PopoCafe / "Popocafepetl" Prague: the benchmark profile

### Takeaway
The venue is **"Popocafepetl"**, a pun on the Popocatépetl volcano. It is a small Prague chain of cash-only café-bars and music clubs that has run since the 1990s. Its reputation rests on student-level prices: reported shots ~25 CZK (~€1), rum-cola/longdrinks 39–65 CZK (~€1.60–2.70) and beer ~25–30 CZK (~€1.00–1.25). Almost all of these figures are several years old and no current 2025/2026 menu could be found. The benchmark should therefore read **"historically ~€1–3 per drink, cash only, crowded student dance bar"**, with a clear caveat that 2026 prices are unverified.

### Inferences
- **The benchmark "price level"** for the report, as historically reported: shot ≈ €1, longdrink/cocktail ≈ €1.60–2.70, beer ≈ €1.00–1.25. In HUF at the cross rate, that is a shot ≈ 375 HUF, a longdrink ≈ 585–975 HUF and a beer ≈ 375–450 HUF **[estimate]**.
- Prague pub prices have risen a lot since most of these reviews were written. Popo's 2026 prices are therefore very likely **higher** than these figures. The real advantage over a typical Prague club, where a cocktail costs ~€8, is probably still large. **[estimate, unverified]**
- **The benchmark "vibe"**: small, loud, crowded, cash-only, with a young student/Erasmus crowd, DJ or live music and spontaneous dancing rather than a proper club floor. People go for the cheap drinks, not the drink quality. It works as a pre-party or starting bar.

## 3. Budapest drink prices 2025–2026, service charge, payment, fees

### Takeaway
In 2026 a **0.5 l draught beer costs ~800–1,000 HUF (€2.20–2.75) in a real local kocsma**, ~1,200–1,600 HUF (€3.30–4.40) in a normal District VII ruin bar such as Instant-Fogas, and **2,200–2,500 HUF (€6.05–6.90) in Szimpla Kert or Gozsdu udvar**. Cocktails cost 2,500–3,500 HUF (€6.90–9.60) in tourist zones and up to 4,000–5,500 HUF at Szimpla. Pálinka shots run 500–800 HUF (€1.40–2.20) and fröccs 400–550 HUF (€1.10–1.50). Prices rose again in 2026 with inflation-indexed excise duty. A 10–15% service charge appears on many bills (legal maximum 15% for private customers) and must be printed on the receipt. Cards are widely accepted. Budapest's tourist tax applies to accommodation, not bar bills.

## 4. Logistics: distances, transport, taxis, safety and District VII opening-hour rules

### Takeaway
The District VII party core is compact: Kazinczy, Akácfa, Dob, Klauzál tér, Király utca and Gozsdu udvar are all within roughly 5–10 minutes' walk of each other. Morrison's 2 (Szent István krt. 11, near Nyugati and Jászai Mari tér) is about a 25–30 minute walk from Szimpla, or a few stops on tram 4/6. Ráday utca (IX) is about 15–20 minutes' walk south. **Important for early Nov 2026: trams 4/6 do NOT run 0:00–4:00 from 28 Sep to 20 Nov 2026 because of maintenance; replacement bus "4-6" runs instead.** The metro runs until about midnight on weekdays and about 1 am at weekends, and a new night bus network has operated since July 2026. Taxi fares rose on 1 Aug 2026 to 1,300 HUF base + ~520 HUF/km. **Bars do not all have to close at midnight.** Midnight is the default in Inner Erzsébetváros, but permitted venues stay open later, and no 2026 rule change or referendum has reversed that.
